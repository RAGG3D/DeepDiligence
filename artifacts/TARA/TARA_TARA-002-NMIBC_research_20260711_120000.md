# TARA-002-NMIBC — Protara Therapeutics (TARA)

Research cutoff: 2026-07-11. This report uses Protara's 2025 Form 10-K, current company releases and presentation, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA approval materials, and named peer-company disclosures. Unknown values are shown as `/` or `NR`.

### Chapter 1: Drug Overview

TARA-002 is an investigational, intravesically administered, inactivated *Streptococcus pyogenes* cell therapy manufactured from the same master cell bank as OK-432. It is designed to activate innate and adaptive immune responses through TLR2/NOD2-linked immunopotentiation and immunogenic tumor-cell death. The lead NMIBC study is ADVANCED-2 (NCT05951179), an open-label Phase 2 trial in high-risk carcinoma in situ with BCG-naive and BCG-unresponsive cohorts. Protara has sole development and commercialization responsibility worldwide excluding Japan and Taiwan; the DCF models the U.S. opportunity at 100% economic share.

**Target:** TLR2/NOD2 innate and adaptive immune activation

Economic share: 100%

Sources: Protara 2025 Form 10-K filed 2026-03-10; ADVANCED-2 NCT05951179; Protara corporate presentation May 2026.

### Chapter 2: Forecasting Strategy

BCG-unresponsive CIS and BCG-naive high-risk CIS receive separate forecasts because their standards of care, competitors and registrational paths differ. Papillary-only expansion and systemic/subcutaneous priming are recorded for completeness but explicitly excluded because they remain exploratory without an independently financeable clinical efficacy dataset or defined approval path.

#### 3.1.3 Competitive Landscape

Inlexzo is the efficacy leader among approved bladder-sparing therapies, with FDA-reported 82% any-time CR and 51% of responders maintaining response for at least 12 months. ANKTIVA plus BCG delivered 62% any-time CR and durable responses but requires BCG. Adstiladrin delivered 51% CR with quarterly dosing. TARA-002's 68.2% six-month landmark CR, 65.7% any-time CR and favorable safety support a differentiated position, but its 12-month evaluable sample is immature and its any-time response is below Inlexzo.

Sources: FDA Inlexzo approval dated 2025-09-09; FDA ANKTIVA approval dated 2024-04-22; FDA Adstiladrin label; Protara ASCO GU release dated 2026-02-23.

#### 3.1.5 Differentiation Assessment

Assessment: Tier One — differentiated BCG-free efficacy and tolerability, but efficacy does not lead Inlexzo and the late-stage field is crowded.

TARA-002 is BCG-free, office-administered and reported no grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events in the January 28, 2026 dataset. Its six-month landmark efficacy is competitive with ANKTIVA and Adstiladrin, but Inlexzo has a higher any-time CR and longer mature follow-up. The modeled share therefore remains single digit in the base case.

#### 3.2.3 Competitive Landscape

BCG induction and maintenance remains the entrenched standard. Pfizer's Phase 3 CREST trial reported event-free survival HR 0.68 for sasanlimab plus BCG versus BCG alone and 36-month event-free probabilities of 82.1% versus 74.8%. Intravesical gemcitabine/docetaxel is widely used off label, while ANKTIVA and Inlexzo are pursuing earlier-line expansion. TARA-002 reported 72.4% any-time CR, 66.7% six-month landmark CR and 55.0% twelve-month landmark CR in the April 5, 2026 data cut.

Sources: Protara AUA release dated 2026-05-15; Pfizer CREST release dated 2025-04-26; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05951179.

#### 3.2.5 Differentiation Assessment

Assessment: Tier One — clinically competitive and well tolerated, with a meaningful but capped earlier-line opportunity pending randomized confirmation.

The 55% twelve-month landmark CR and absence of grade 3 or higher treatment-related adverse events are encouraging. However, the evidence is single arm, BCG remains low cost and familiar, and ADVANCED-3 has not yet generated randomized data.
