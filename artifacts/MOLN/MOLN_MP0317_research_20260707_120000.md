### Chapter 1: Drug Overview
MP0317 is Molecular Partners' tumor-localized immuno-oncology DARPin designed to activate CD40 only in FAP-positive tumor stroma. It is a bispecific FAP x CD40 agonist intended to concentrate CD40 stimulation in the tumor microenvironment and avoid the systemic toxicity that limited earlier CD40 antibodies. MP0317 completed a Phase 1 advanced solid tumor study and is now in a Phase 2 investigator-initiated study in advanced biliary tract cancer/cholangiocarcinoma in combination with durvalumab plus gemcitabine/cisplatin.
**Target:** FAP x CD40 tumor-localized CD40 agonist

### Chapter 2: Forecasting Strategy
MP0317 is modeled in advanced biliary tract cancer/cholangiocarcinoma (BTC) only. The active Phase 2 study is in BTC, and broader FAP-positive solid-tumor optionality is kept out of the DCF until a dedicated active clinical program or efficacy dataset supports a separate indication.

#### 3.1.3 Competitive Landscape
Marketed:
- Durvalumab + gemcitabine/cisplatin (Imfinzi regimen) — first-line advanced BTC standard after TOPAZ-1 showed OS benefit versus gemcitabine/cisplatin alone.
- Pembrolizumab + gemcitabine/cisplatin (Keytruda regimen) — first-line advanced BTC option after KEYNOTE-966 showed OS benefit.
- FGFR2, IDH1 and HER2 targeted therapies — molecularly selected later-line options; they do not directly compete with a first-line add-on immunostimulatory approach.

Clinical-stage:
- CD40 agonist and myeloid/TME-modulating approaches in solid tumors have shown biologic rationale but inconsistent efficacy. MP0317's FAP-localized CD40 activation is differentiated mechanistically, but BTC-specific randomized data are not available yet.

#### 3.1.5 Differentiation
MP0317's rationale is tumor-localized CD40 activation on top of IO-chemo, which may deepen antigen presentation and T-cell priming while avoiding systemic CD40 toxicity. It is not yet head-to-head superior to the established durvalumab/gemcitabine/cisplatin or pembrolizumab/gemcitabine/cisplatin backbones, and no randomized BTC efficacy dataset has been disclosed. Net assessment: **Average** until Phase 2 BTC response, PFS, and safety data prove incremental benefit over IO-chemo.
